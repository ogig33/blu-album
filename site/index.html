Album player for BluOS devices

A single HTML file to browse and play the local music library of a BluOS player (Bluesound, NAD) in your browser. Albums are shown by their covers; you find them with search, filters and sorting.

Features

Browse

Albums of the network share and the USB drive, loaded while you scroll.

Search

Album titles, track artists, album artists, composers.

Filter

All, favourites, new, quality (HD, CD, MQA), decades, genres, playlists.

Sort

By album title, album artist, release year or randomly.

Album details

Tracks, year, playing time, format, bit depth, sample rate, bitrate, discs.

Quick actions

Play, add to the queue, mark as favourite; random album from any filter.

Playlists

BluOS and library playlists: play, add to the queue, list the tracks; favourite library playlists also in Favourites.

Queue and player bar

Shuffle, repeat, reorder, remove; play / pause, seek, volume, listening mode.

Full screen and design

Current cover in full screen; light, dark or system design; cover size.

Use it

  1. Download

    blualbum.html from the latest release.

  2. Open

    Open the file in your browser. Nothing is installed. Phones and tablets open it from a web server in your home network, e.g. a NAS.

  3. Connect

    Enter the IP address of your player, e.g. 192.168.1.50 (BluOS Controller app: Settings › Diagnostics).

BluAlbum talks to the player directly in your home network (http://<player>:11000). Only two things leave your network: Check for updates in the about popup (on click) and the image of what is playing, when the player takes it from the internet (e.g. radio). Favourites and playlists are stored on the player.

Requirements

  • A BluOS player in your local network
  • A local music library on a network share or USB drive, tagged and with cover art (library guidance)
  • A current browser on a computer: Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox on macOS, Windows or Linux; or Safari on iPhone and iPad

Not included

  • Streaming services: use their apps with Connect
  • Radio, inputs, presets, player settings: use the BluOS Controller app
